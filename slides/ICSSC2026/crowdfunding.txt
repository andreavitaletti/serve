A Few Notes on Delivering This Well
On visuals: the micro-greenhouse itself needs to look beautiful in the video — this is non-negotiable. Its design is doing persuasive work. If you don't have a physical prototype yet, a well-rendered 3D model or even a Canva illustration works fine at this stage.
On the voiceover: the tone should feel like a nature documentary crossed with a TED talk — calm authority, not hype. ElevenLabs has voices that hit this register perfectly. Avoid anything that sounds like a commercial.
On music: look for something minimal and slightly hopeful on Pixabay Music — piano or acoustic guitar, not orchestral swells. The credibility comes from restraint.
On length: the pauses between scenes matter as much as the words. Don't rush it.
